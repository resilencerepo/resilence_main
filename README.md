## 🎵 Project Context

> This repository is part of the **ReSilence – Retune the Soundscape of Future Cities through Art and Science Collaboration** project.
> 
> ReSilence is funded by the European Union under the **STARTS – Science, Technology & the Arts** initiative, supporting innovation at the intersection of science, technology, and the arts. The project explores how artistic experimentation, AI technologies, and multisensory design can reshape urban soundscapes and enhance future city experiences.

* **Learn more:** [resilence.eu](https://resilence.eu/)
* **STARTS Initiative:** [starts.eu](https://starts.eu/)
